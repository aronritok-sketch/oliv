Corporate & Athlete Yoga

Office yoga, team-building sessions, lunchtime classes and yoga for athletes and sports teams across Southwest Florida.

✺

Office yoga and team building

If you'd like to improve your team's focus, stress management and mental resilience — or you're simply a group of people who love yoga — let's plan something memorable together. A one-off team-building session, a weekly class alongside lunch, or short desk-friendly resets built into the working day.

Formats

  • Team-building session: 60–90 minutes, any fitness level, on-site or outdoors.
  • Weekly lunchtime class: 45–60 minutes, in a meeting room or on the lawn.
  • Chair and desk yoga: 20–30 minute resets for neck, shoulders and back. No mats, no changing clothes.
  • Retreats and offsites: sunrise flows or evening wind-downs for your event.

What teams usually notice

Less stiffness from long hours at a screen, easier breathing under pressure, and a shared calm that carries into meetings. And it's simply a good hour spent together.

Yoga for athletes and sports teams

If you'd like to improve your team's or your athletes' mobility, concentration, stress management, breathing and endurance, let's add yoga to training — with a strength, mobility or recovery focus — so everyone can bring 100% to the match, the race or the competition.

  • Mobility sessions for hips, shoulders and spine
  • Breathing practice for focus and faster recovery
  • Recovery classes after heavy training blocks
  • Pre-season and in-season programs
