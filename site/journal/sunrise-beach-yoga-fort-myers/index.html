Sunrise Beach Yoga Around Fort Myers: How to Make It Feel Amazing

Practical tips for practicing yoga on the sand in Southwest Florida — timing, heat, tides, what to bring and how to make the most of it.

September 8, 20262 min readBeach yoga
✺

There are few better places to practice yoga than a Southwest Florida beach before the day heats up. The sand is cool, the Gulf is often glassy, and the pelicans are already at work. Here's how to make a beach session feel as good as it looks.

Go early (or late)

From late spring to early fall, the first hour after sunrise is the sweet spot: cooler air, softer light, fewer people. Because our Gulf beaches face west, the sun rises behind you — which means gentle light on your back rather than glare in your eyes. Sunset sessions are beautiful too, with the sun going down over the water, but the sand holds the day's heat longer.

Choose your spot

Fort Myers Beach, Sanibel, Lovers Key and Bonita Beach all have long stretches of sand. Look for firm, flat sand just above the high-tide line: dry enough to stay put, firm enough for balance poses. Check the tide times before you go so the water doesn't join the class. If you're planning an organized group session in a park or preserve, check the local rules first.

What to bring

  • A large towel or an old mat (sand gets everywhere — don't bring your favorite)
  • Water, even at 7am
  • Reef-safe sunscreen and a hat for the way back
  • A light layer for the final relaxation on breezy mornings

Adjust your practice to the sand

Sand is unstable, which makes balance poses both harder and more rewarding. Widen your stance a little, move more slowly, and let your feet work — they'll thank you. Keep flows gentle and save the strongest standing work for the studio. Lying down at the end and listening to the waves is the best relaxation you'll ever have.

Want company?

I teach sunrise and sunset sessions on the beach as private yoga for individuals, couples, families and groups — and from time to time as open events. It's a lovely way to start a vacation, celebrate a birthday or simply reset a busy week.
