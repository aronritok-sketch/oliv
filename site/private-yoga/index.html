Private Yoga in Fort Myers

One-on-one yoga at your home, lanai, condo clubhouse, on the beach or online — planned around your body and your goals.

✺

A class that fits exactly one person

In a group class I teach to the room. In a private session I teach to you: your shoulders after years at a desk, the knee you're careful with, the golf swing you'd like back, the sleep you'd like to find. We work at your pace, and every session builds on the last.

Who private yoga is for

  • Complete beginners who want to learn the foundations safely before joining a group
  • Anyone who prefers to practice without an audience
  • People working around stiffness, old injuries or limited mobility — with their doctor's OK
  • Couples, friends and families who want to practice together
  • Busy people who'd rather yoga came to them
  • Visitors and seasonal residents who want a sunrise session during their stay

Where we can practice

At home or on your lanai. All we need is a quiet spot the size of a yoga mat — or two, if you'd like to practice with a partner. I can bring mats and props.

On the beach. Sunrise and sunset sessions on the sand around Fort Myers Beach, Sanibel, Bonita Beach or your local stretch of the Gulf. In the summer months, early mornings are cooler and calmer.

In your condo or HOA clubhouse. A private group class for your community, one-off or weekly.

Online. Live, one-on-one video sessions — useful when you travel, or between in-person classes.

How it works

  1. Send a message with your area and the days that suit you.
  2. We talk for 15 minutes about how you move, any injuries or conditions, and what you'd like to feel after a class.
  3. Your first session. I bring a plan and adjust it as we go. You'll leave with one or two simple practices to do on your own.
  4. Keep going with single sessions or a 5-session pack — weekly, or whenever it suits you.

Sessions and prices

Private sessions are 60, 75 or 90 minutes. You'll find current rates on the schedule and pricing page.
