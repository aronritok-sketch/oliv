Slow Flow

Slower and more observant. We still flow, but with time to feel each shape — ideal after a long day and for beginners.

75–90 minBeginners welcomeIntensity: Gentle
✺

A slower class with more room to notice

Slow Flow still moves — the flow experience matters to me in every class — but the pace leaves space to feel each shape, to notice where you're holding on, and to let go a little. There's more time on the floor, more stretching and a longer relaxation.

Who it's for

Complete beginners, anyone returning after a break, people who sit a lot, and anyone who ends the day both wired and tired. Experienced practitioners love it too, as a counterweight to stronger training.

What to expect

  • Gentle breathwork to arrive
  • Mobility for the spine, hips and shoulders
  • Slow, mindful standing and balance work
  • Longer-held floor stretches
  • A guided relaxation to close
